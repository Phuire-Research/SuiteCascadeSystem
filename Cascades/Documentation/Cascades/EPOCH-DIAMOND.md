# Epoch Diamond — Cascade Structure

**Status**: Formalized 2026-05-30. The pattern was used *indirectly* across prior cycles; this document **names and constrains** it. To be folded into the SuiteCascade Documentation Page (Macro B · Band B-6 HCD · the Documentation SubPage) as one of the catalogued Diamond forms + Commands.

---

## §1 · Definition

An **Epoch Diamond** is a composition of **N Macro Diamonds (N ≥ 2)** whose combined lead is **foundation-building that bridges into a newest, yet-undefined Macro Diamond** — and, in the general case, into a **new Epoch**.

### The Cascade depth ladder

```
Sub-Diamond  <  Diamond  <  Macro Diamond  <  Epoch Diamond
```

- A **Diamond** composes sub-Diamonds toward one goal (the 8-Gate cycle).
- A **Macro Diamond** composes sub-Diamonds across a sustained goal (e.g. the Relay Refinement Macro = RM-D1..D4).
- An **Epoch Diamond** composes **N Macro Diamonds (N ≥ 2)** whose lead **bridges into** a new Macro Diamond / new Epoch.

---

## §2 · The Bridge / Lead

The Epoch Diamond's substance is the **lead** from the N Macro Diamonds **into** the newest Macro Diamond. That lead is **foundation-building**: it builds what the final Macro Diamond needs in order to be actualized — **without focusing on the specifics the foundation will build**. Those specifics emerge *in the final Macro Diamond itself*, not in the foundation.

The Epoch Diamond is about the **bridge**, not the exhaustive depth of any one sub-Diamond.

---

## §3 · Constrained Scope — "Do Not Get Lost in the Field of Poppies"

The Epoch Diamond **deliberately constrains its own scope**. Within it we may **rush through** the sub-Diamonds of each constituent Macro Diamond **without addressing their full scope** — laying the foundation and the bridge rather than completing every sub-Diamond. Each sub-Diamond **dives further in its own Lambda** when later cascaded.

The **field of poppies** (Wizard of Oz) names the failure mode: getting lost in sub-Diamond depth before the bridge is laid — exhausting every Macro and actualizing nothing. The Epoch Diamond's discipline is the inverse: **foundation + bridge first; depth in Lambda.**

---

## §4 · Continuation

An Epoch Diamond **can continue to lead into new Macro Diamonds**, and is designed to **lead into new Epochs**. One Epoch's bridge target (a Macro Diamond) can itself become the foundation of the next Epoch Diamond — Epochs chain.

---

## §5 · The Command

**`Engage Epoch`** — composes the constituent Macro Diamonds as foundation, then lays the bridge into the (possibly yet-undefined) target Macro Diamond, under the §3 field-of-poppies scope constraint.
