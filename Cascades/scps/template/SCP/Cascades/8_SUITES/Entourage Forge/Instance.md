## Etymology

**Entourage** — the retinue: the Forge never works alone; it leads the Suite 8s that
accompany a creation (Cadmium researches, the Bridge grounds, the Template lends its shape).
**Forge** — where the cast is made: a named blank enters; a tempered, domain-bearing
Suite 8 leaves. The Forge conducts heat and hands — it does not become the blade.
