# Cascade Diamond Forms — The Initial Reference

The catalogued Diamond forms and their **Commands** — the command set built
through this whole process is the first Documentation entry.

## The Cascade Depth Ladder

```
Sub-Diamond  <  Diamond  <  Macro Diamond  <  Epoch Diamond
```

| Form | Composes | Toward |
|---|---|---|
| **Sub-Diamond** | a single named pattern | one facet of a Diamond's goal |
| **Diamond** | sub-Diamonds | one goal (the 8-Gate cycle) |
| **Macro Diamond** | sub-Diamonds | a sustained goal (e.g. a Relay Refinement Macro) |
| **Epoch Diamond** | N Macro Diamonds (N >= 2) | a bridge into a newest, yet-undefined Macro Diamond / new Epoch |

## The Bridge / Lead

The Epoch Diamond's substance is the **lead** from the N Macro Diamonds **into**
the newest Macro Diamond. That lead is **foundation-building** — it builds what the
final Macro Diamond needs to be actualized, without focusing on the specifics the
foundation will build. Those specifics emerge in the final Macro Diamond itself.

## Constrained Scope — "Do Not Get Lost in the Field of Poppies"

The Epoch Diamond deliberately constrains its own scope: rush through the
sub-Diamonds of each constituent Macro **without addressing their full scope** —
laying the foundation and the bridge rather than completing every sub-Diamond.
Each sub-Diamond **dives further in its own Lambda** when later cascaded.

> **Field of poppies** (Wizard of Oz) names the failure mode: getting lost in
> sub-Diamond depth before the bridge is laid. The discipline is the inverse:
> **foundation + bridge first; depth in Lambda.**

## Continuation

An Epoch Diamond **can continue to lead into new Macro Diamonds**, and is designed
to **lead into new Epochs**. One Epoch's bridge target (a Macro Diamond) can itself
become the foundation of the next Epoch Diamond — Epochs chain.

## The Commands

| Command | Effect |
|---|---|
| **`Engage Epoch`** | Composes the constituent Macro Diamonds as foundation, then lays the bridge into the (possibly yet-undefined) target Macro Diamond, under the field-of-poppies scope constraint. |

The Magic ShotGun foundation salvo that grounds each constituent Diamond's planning
arc is formalized in `MAGIC-SHOTGUN.md`.
